Did You Know?

The Incredible Homing Pigeon

The world of pigeon racing is stranger and more amazing than you'd ever guess. Here are a few things Woot could tell you…

🧭

Built-in GPS

Homing pigeons can find their way home from over 1,300 miles away — using Earth's magnetic field, the sun, and even familiar landmarks.

💨

Lightning Fast

Racing pigeons can reach speeds of up to 90 mph. The average race pace is 50–60 mph — sustained over hundreds of miles.

🪖

War Heroes

Pigeons carried messages in both World Wars. One famous bird, Cher Ami, delivered a critical message that saved nearly 200 trapped soldiers in WWI.

🪞

Self-Aware

Pigeons can recognize themselves in a mirror — a cognitive ability shared by only a handful of animals, including great apes, dolphins, and elephants.

❤️

Loyal for Life

Pigeons mate for life. Both parents share the work of building the nest, incubating eggs, and feeding their young.

👁️

Nearly 360° Vision

A pigeon's eyes are placed on the sides of its head, giving it a visual field of almost 340 degrees — perfect for spotting predators mid-flight.

Our Story

Twelve-year-old Daniel Wilson shares an unusual hobby with his father, Jed. They breed and race homing pigeons! But father and son don't see eye to eye on the purpose of the birds. Jed views the birds as competitors — he needs to win his bet on which bird will come in first in every race. But Daniel has a special gift. He understands the birds and comes to discover he can even talk to them! In fact, his star racing pigeon — Woot — becomes his secret best friend. As Daniel and Jed prepare Woot for the Southwest Regional Pigeon Race, everything is on the line. Will Woot win and prove to be the champion Daniel knows he is? Or will he lose the race and be cast off, like so many other pigeons who don't become racing stars? The stakes are high for both boy and bird as this desert adventure unfolds.
